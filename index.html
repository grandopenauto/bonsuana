Great things don't always start with a business plan. Sometimes they start with a sip that makes you stop and say, “Wait. That's amazing.”

Bonsuana was born from an unexpected flavor experiment — tart cherry, orange, cranberry-raspberry lemonade, and horchata coming together in one wonderfully unconventional drink.

Now the idea is growing into something bigger: a bold, curious brand built around good flavor, memorable moments, and the freedom to show up somewhere unexpected.

✳ THE NEED-TO-KNOW

GOOD
QUESTIONS.
GREAT TASTE.

Curious? We like that about you.

What exactly is Bonsuana Juice?+

A beverage and experiential brand concept centered on an original four-part drink and the creative experiences we hope to build around it.

What's in the original blend?+

Equal parts tart cherry juice, orange juice, cranberry-raspberry lemonade, and horchata. Preparation and ingredients may be adjusted for service; always ask about allergens.

Can I buy it right now?+

We're developing the brand and exploring pop-ups and partnerships. No retail products, service dates, or ordering are being advertised on this site yet.

Are you available for events?+

We're interested in conversations about future pop-ups, hospitality, tastings, and collaborations. Any event arrangement would depend on timing, permitting, and operational readiness.

Where is Bonsuana based?+

The idea has Northern California roots, with a mobile, wherever-the-moment-takes-us outlook.
